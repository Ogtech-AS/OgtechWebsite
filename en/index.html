How it works

From air to fish – no tanker truck

The oxygen is made where it is used, by a plant installed at or next to your farm.

  1. Air inThe plant draws in ordinary ambient air.
  2. Oxygen is separatedPSA, VSA or VPSA technology separates the oxygen from the air, on site.
  3. Straight to the fishThe oxygen goes directly to production, with no transport.

Backup: oxygen from a tank, gas cylinders or the same technology – and power from battery, hybrid or diesel.

Monitoring: sensors and communication follow the plant around the clock, and error messages go straight to your phone.

Frequently asked questions

What most people ask

What happens during a power outage?

The plant can be delivered with power backup from battery, hybrid or diesel, from small units up to 1000 kVA. In addition, oxygen backup from a tank, gas cylinders or the same technology can take over.

Do we need our own staff with oxygen expertise?

No. The plants are designed to be easy to operate, and where advanced settings are needed, we help via remote control. OgTech Core is being developed to make the operator's job even easier.

Can we keep our existing LOX tank?

Yes. We deliver solutions that work together with LOX or gas cylinders.

Where is the plant installed?

In a container, on a feed barge, in a machine room or on deck – or in your own premises next to fish production.

How large a plant do we need?

We deliver from 10 to 20,000 kg of oxygen per day, and size the plant to your consumption. Get in touch and we will run the numbers.

How do we keep track of operation?

The plants come with sensors and communication as standard. This gives around-the-clock monitoring, with error messages sent automatically to your phone. OgTech Core is being developed to bring status and operating data together in one place.
