Privacy

Privacy policy for OGTECH AS. Last updated 27 January 2024.

This is an English translation. If there is any difference between the versions, the Norwegian version applies.

OGTECH AS is the data controller for the processing of personal data as described in this privacy policy. In this privacy policy we explain what kind of personal data we store and how we process it. This privacy policy applies to: https://ogtech.no/

Personal data we collect and process

We process the following categories of personal data:

  • Basic information: name, date of birth
  • Contact information: address, phone number, email address
  • Customer activity: reading and action history from apps, websites or electronic communication we send out, as well as technical information about the devices you use.

The personal data is collected directly from you through information you provide to us, and in connection with your use of our services and products.

How we use personal data

Providing services and entering into agreements

We use your personal data to fulfil our agreements with you, that is, when you have ordered a product or a service from us. The legal basis for processing personal data for this purpose is that the processing is necessary to fulfil an agreement with you.

Managing customer relationships

We use your personal data to manage our customer relationship with you. This may for example be customer service, complaint handling and error correction relating to your customer relationship. The legal basis for processing personal data for this purpose is that the processing is necessary to fulfil an agreement with you.

Analysis, business development and service improvement

We work continuously to develop and improve our services and products. Much of this work involves analysing various forms of personal data, such as customer activity, customer history and account and profile information. The legal basis for processing personal data for this purpose is our legitimate interest.

Your rights

If you wish to exercise any of your rights, contact us at kristian@ogtech.no.

Right of access to your own data

You can ask for a copy of all the data we process about you. Contact us at the email address above to exercise your right of access.

Right to rectification of personal data

You have the right to ask us to correct or supplement data that is incorrect or misleading.

Right to erasure of personal data

You have the right to have your personal data erased without undue delay. You can therefore ask us to delete data about you at any time. Please note that information we are required to keep because of other legal obligations (such as the Norwegian Bookkeeping Act) will not be deleted.

Restriction of processing of personal data

In some situations you can also ask us to restrict the processing of data about you. You do this by managing consents or reservations in our solutions.

Objecting to the processing of personal data

If we process data about you on the basis of our tasks or on the basis of a balancing of interests, you have the right to object to our processing of data about you. You do this by managing consents or reservations in our solutions.

Data portability

You have the right to receive your personal data in a structured, commonly used and machine-readable format. Contact us at the email address above to receive your personal data.

You can complain about our processing of personal data

We hope you will let us know if you believe we are not complying with the rules of the Norwegian Personal Data Act. Please first raise it through the contact or channel you have already established with us. You can also complain about our processing of personal data to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority (Datatilsynet).
