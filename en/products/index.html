Oxygen plants

Custom oxygen plants, from 10 to 20,000 kg of oxygen per day.

On-site oxygen production based on PSA or VPSA technology is the most cost-effective oxygen solution for aquaculture. A production plant installed in the customer's premises, or in a container or cabinet connected to fish production, quickly pays for itself compared with solutions that rely on transport.

Ogtech offers solutions based on both PSA and VPSA technology, from 10 to 20,000 kg of oxygen per day. In our applications we use solutions that are reliable and at the same time give a low life-cycle cost per kg of oxygen produced. Atlas Copco's patented technology and our own solutions give us advantages over competitors.

Our systems are adapted to aquaculture. The solution can be supplied with oxygen control and energy recovery, according to the customer's wishes and needs.

Control and automation

OgTechCore

A user-friendly control system. Status, pressure, temperature, flow and purity in one place.

  • Around-the-clock monitoringError messages are sent automatically to the operator's phone.
  • Available anywhereAll data and alerts are stored and available via 4G/5G.
  • Safe operationStep-by-step start-up and shutdown, with automatic checks of conditions.
  • Remote assistanceWhere advanced settings are needed, we assist directly via remote control.

Good service and maintenance routines are a prerequisite for a long life and trouble-free operation of oxygen plants. Sensors and analysis of collected data, together with communication systems, are standard equipment in our plants. This ensures 24/7 monitoring, with error messages sent automatically to the operator's mobile phone and other preferred channels.

The control system

With OgTechCore, the operator gets a complete overview of status, pressure, temperature, flow and purity in one place. Clear key figures make deviations easy to spot and quick to resolve, and all data and alerts are stored and available outside the machine room via 4G/5G.

The system guides the operator through the relevant choices for start, pause, resume and stop based on the plant's current status. Step-by-step start-up and shutdown lead the user safely through the process and minimise the risk of errors. Conditions are checked automatically before each action, making operation simpler, safer and more consistent – and where advanced settings are required, we assist directly via remote control.

OgTechCore is developed to provide a complete operating solution that requires no prior knowledge and minimal training.
